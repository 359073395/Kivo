# Source policy and boundaries

## Evidence levels

Use these labels on every material claim:

1. `原著支撑`：traceable to the 1991 four-volume scanned text. OCR is a locator; visually recheck exact quotations.
2. `解读/课程观点`：a document author or lecturer's interpretation. It may inspire a hypothesis but cannot rewrite the original.
3. `AI迁移推断`：a modern decision mechanism derived from the first two levels. It must state scope, risk and disconfirming evidence.
4. `当前事实`：verified facts from the present decision. Current facts outrank historical analogy.

## Authority order

For source disputes: scanned four-volume pages > searchable combined edition > interpretation documents > video courses > AI inference. For present-day legal, platform, medical or financial facts, current authoritative sources outrank every item above.

## Transfer rule

Transfer a mechanism only when it has all of the following:

- a present trigger;
- observable inputs;
- an action that can be performed before the result;
- a prediction or success signal;
- a counterexample or failure signal;
- a stop/review condition.

Reject slogans, unlimited perseverance, dehumanizing labels, and explanations that can fit every possible result.
