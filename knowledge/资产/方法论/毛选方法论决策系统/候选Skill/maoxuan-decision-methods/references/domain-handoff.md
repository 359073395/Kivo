# Domain handoff

This candidate analyzes the decision structure; active domain Skills own execution details.

- Personal IP, positioning, commercial path or content matrix: hand off to `ip-commerce-decision-system` after producing facts, priority and stage assumptions.
- TikTok Shop product selection, SKU, listing or weekly operations: hand off to `tiktok-commerce-operations`.
- TikTok scripts, content factory, HeyGen or ChatCut production: hand off to `tiktok-content-system` and the relevant production capability.
- Creator screening and outreach preparation: hand off to `tiktok-creator-outreach`.
- Learning a new source or changing this candidate: hand off to `knowledge-distillation`; rule/manifest maintenance goes through `knowledge-iteration`.

The handoff packet must contain: objective, verified facts, unknowns, selected main contradiction, stage, resource ceiling, success/failure signals and stop conditions. The receiving Skill may reject the diagnosis when current domain evidence conflicts with it.

As a course-layer audit (not original wording), a usable decision package needs four distinct inputs: a general reasoning frame, current domain knowledge, an executable playbook, and investigated facts for this case. Missing any one blocks confident execution; this candidate may structure the question but must not fabricate the missing domain layer.
