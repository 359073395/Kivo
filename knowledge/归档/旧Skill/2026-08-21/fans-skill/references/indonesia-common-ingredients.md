# Indonesia Common Ingredient Rules

Use this reference before generating Indonesian health, home-remedy, kitchen,
drink, food, or plant-based follower-growth videos.

## Hard Rule

Every food, plant, herb, spice, drink, or kitchen ingredient in the script,
top hook, subtitles, caption, visual concept, image prompt, filename slug, and
manifest must be common and recognizable in Indonesia.

If an ingredient is not obviously common in Indonesian daily kitchens, pasar,
warung, jamu culture, or home-remedy content, replace it before generation.
Do not keep an uncommon ingredient just because it sounds healthy or trendy.

## Preferred Common Ingredients

Use these first for natural-remedy and wellness-style static videos:

- Spices and roots: jahe, kunyit, temulawak, kencur, lengkuas, serai,
  kayu manis, cengkeh, ketumbar, jintan, kapulaga, pala, merica.
- Leaves and aromatics: daun pandan, daun salam, daun jeruk, daun kelor,
  daun mint, kemangi, seledri, bawang putih, bawang merah.
- Fruits: jeruk nipis, lemon, pepaya, pisang, mangga, jambu biji, nanas,
  belimbing, alpukat, kelapa, semangka, melon, apel.
- Vegetables and simple kitchen items: mentimun, wortel, tomat, labu,
  ubi jalar, jagung, okra, kacang hijau, kacang merah, beras, beras merah.
- Drinks and traditional bases: air hangat, air kelapa, madu, asam jawa,
  gula aren, santan, teh, teh rosella, bunga telang, jamu-style warm drinks.

## Use With Caution

These may be recognized by some Indonesian audiences but should not dominate a
batch unless the concept clearly justifies them:

- kurma: common around Ramadan and as a sweetener, but not an everyday local
  plant.
- almond, chia seed, oat, quinoa, kale, cranberry, blueberry, goji berry,
  matcha: imported or niche wellness ingredients; avoid for default fans videos.
- saffron, ginseng, maca, echinacea, milk thistle: too foreign or niche for the
  0703 everyday-kitchen style.

## Batch Quality Gate

Before finalizing a batch:

1. List every ingredient used in all hooks, subtitles, captions, image prompts,
   slugs, and manifests.
2. Confirm each one is in the preferred common list or clearly common in
   Indonesia.
3. Replace questionable items with a local alternative.
4. Keep the visual familiar: kitchen table, wooden board, glass cup, local
   fruit/herb/spice, simple home background.

## Good Replacement Examples

- Replace `chia seed` with `biji selasih`, `daun kelor`, or `kacang hijau`
  when the topic allows.
- Replace `almond milk` with `santan`, `susu kedelai`, or `air kelapa`.
- Replace `cranberry` or `blueberry` with `jambu biji`, `nanas`, `pepaya`, or
  `mangga`.
- Replace `kale` with `daun kelor`, `bayam`, or `kangkung`.
